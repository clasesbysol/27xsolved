Lo que hacés siempre: rutinas, costumbres

03 · PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Present continuous

Lo que está pasando ahora

I stand in the present and I watch the action. It is happening now. Estoy en el presente y miro la acción mientras pasa.

Form Cómo se forma: am / is / are + verbo con -ing

Example

05 · PAST SIMPLE

Past simple

Lo que pasó y terminó

I stand in the present and I look at the past. Estoy en el presente y miro algo terminado.

Was / were El verbo be en pasado

+I / he / she / it was · you / we / they were
−wasn't · weren't — His paintings weren't popular.
?Where were you born? — I was born in Buenos Aires in 2011.

07 · WHEN / WHILE

Past continuous + past simple

Una acción larga interrumpida por una corta

Like a movie: a long scene (was doing) and something short happens (arrived). Como una película: escena larga + algo corto.

while + long actionWhile Tom was doing his homework, his mum arrived.
when + short actionTom was doing his homework when his mum arrived.

Long action: was / were + -ing. Short action: past simple. While (mientras) va con la larga; when (cuando) va con la corta.

He had the idea while he was travelling with his daughter. · We were walking in the park when we saw an actor.

11 · READING & WRITING

Cómo responder preguntas y cómo escribir

Las preguntas de comprensión se contestan con el mismo tiempo verbal de la pregunta. Y los textos tienen siempre título, inicio, desarrollo y cierre.

Responder preguntas de un texto

  1. Mirá el auxiliar de la pregunta: te dice el tiempo de la respuesta. What does she do…? → present simple. What was he doing…? → past continuous.
  2. Buscá la evidencia en el texto (subrayala). No inventes: la respuesta tiene que estar ahí o deducirse de ahí.
  3. Contestá con oración completa, con sujeto y verbo. Si es de sí/no: respuesta corta + oración.
  4. Si es why: empezá con because y explicá. La profe usa PEE: Point (idea), Evidence (lo que dice el texto), Explain (por qué).
Q: What was Mia doing when she saw the actor?She was looking at a poster.

Pregunta en past continuous → respuesta en past continuous.

Q: Does Mia usually spend her weekends at festivals?No, she doesn't. She usually spends her weekends at home.

Respuesta corta con el mismo auxiliar + oración completa.

Q: Why was Mia nervous?Because she wanted to ask him a question, and he was famous.

Why → because + razón sacada del texto.

Formato 1 · Photo description present continuous booklet p. 6

Estructura: dónde están → quiénes hay (there is / there are) → qué hacen → qué llevan puesto → qué te parece y because.

In this photo there are two friends at a festival. They are standing in front of a big poster and they are smiling. The girl is wearing a pink dress and a hat with ears, and the boy is wearing a black jacket and sunglasses. There are a lot of people behind them, so the place is very busy. I like this photo because they are having fun together.

Estructura: título → topic sentence (quién es y por qué es famoso/a) → nacimiento y familia → tres hechos importantes en orden → logros → cierre con tu opinión.

A young inventor

Laura Medina is a young inventor from Córdoba. She is famous because she designed a cheap water filter for schools. She was born in a small town in 2006. Her mother was a nurse and her father worked in a garage, so she learned to fix things at home.

At the age of twelve, she started a robotics club. In 2021 her team won a national competition. A year later, while she was visiting a rural school, she noticed that the water was dirty. She had an idea and she built the first filter with her friends.

Today she studies engineering and teaches younger students. I think she is brave and hard-working.

12 · ERRORES DE LA CARPETA

Lo que se escribió y cómo se corrige

Estas frases son de la carpeta (a la izquierda, tal cual se escribieron). Son los errores que más se repiten, así que vale la pena mirarlos uno por uno.

Preguntas

Where Messi lives?Where does Messi live?

Falta el auxiliar does; con él, el verbo pierde la -s. WA0039

Does she plays tennis?Does she play tennis?

La -s ya la lleva does.

How the boy was moving in the second part?How was the boy moving in the second part?

Pregunta: el auxiliar was va antes del sujeto. WA0043

Was any another people traveling?Were any other people travelling?

people es plural → were; another es para singular → other. WA0043

Where did he be?Where was he?

Con be nunca se usa did. WA0051

where and when you was born?Where and when were you born?

you va con were y el auxiliar va antes del sujeto. WA0046

Why don't you like doing in your free time?What don't you like doing in your free time?

Se pregunta qué cosa, no por qué. WA0026

Verbos y tiempos

He's combing his hair now but he never doesn't comb it.He's combing his hair now, but he never combs it.

never ya es negativo: no se junta con doesn't. WA0030

She born in New Jersey.She was born in New Jersey.

Nacer = was / were born.

Because he's an actor and the movie get famous.Because he's an actor and the movie became famous.

Pasado: get → got; mejor became (se hizo famosa). WA0046

She thought everybody can swim.She thought everybody could swim.

Si el verbo principal está en pasado, can → could (lo corrigió la profe). WA0042

They are dressing alike.They are wearing similar clothes.

dressing es «vestirse» (la acción); para la ropa que llevan: wearing. WA0028

The park have a lot of grass.The park has a lot of grass.

the park = it → has. WA0028

They sims happy and focus.They seem happy and focused.

Parecer = seem; concentrados = focused. WA0028

Palabritas que se escapan

I like listen music.I like listening to music.

Después de like: verbo con -ing. Y se escucha to algo. WA0024

Because is a really good sport woman.Because she is a really good sportswoman.

Falta el sujeto she. WA0046

…help him with him lessons.…help him with his lessons.

Posesivo: his (su, de él). WA0025

At the afternoon … on evening.In the afternoon … in the evening.

Partes del día: in the morning / afternoon / evening. WA0029

…eats popcorns.…eats popcorn.

popcorn no lleva -s. WA0029

a superhero whit extraordinary powera superhero with extraordinary powers

Ortografía: with. WA0036

They are sitting in a pier.They are sitting on a pier.

Sobre un muelle → on. WA0028

They make publicitis.They make advertisements (adverts).

Publicidad = advertisement (lo anotó la profe). WA0040

Mia at the comic festival

Last Saturday, Mia and her brother went to a comic festival in Buenos Aires. They arrived at ten o'clock. Many people were wearing colourful costumes, and some visitors were taking photos. Mia was looking at a poster when she saw a famous actor. He was talking to a group of fans. Mia wanted to ask him a question, but she was very nervous. Her brother encouraged her, so she walked towards the actor. He smiled and answered her question. Later, they watched a short film and bought a comic book. Mia usually spends her weekends at home, but that Saturday was very different. She wrote about her experience for her English class.

past simplepast continuouspresent simpleconector
  1. Where did Mia and her brother go?
  2. What were people wearing?
  3. What was Mia doing when she saw the actor?
  4. What was the actor doing?
  5. Why was Mia nervous?
  6. Did the actor answer her question?
  7. What did Mia and her brother do later?
  8. Does Mia usually spend her weekends at festivals?
  9. Find three past simple verbs and two past continuous forms.
  10. Explain the meaning of but and so in the text.
Ver respuestas
  1. They went to a comic festival in Buenos Aires.
  2. They were wearing colourful costumes.
  3. She was looking at a poster.
  4. He was talking to a group of fans.
  5. Because she wanted to ask him a question and he was a famous actor. (Respuesta con because y evidencia del texto.)
  6. Yes, he did. He smiled and answered her question.
  7. They watched a short film and bought a comic book.
  8. No, she doesn't. She usually spends her weekends at home.
  9. Past simple: went, arrived, saw (también wanted, walked, bought…). Past continuous: were wearing, was looking (también were taking, was talking).
  10. but marca contraste (quería preguntar, pero estaba nerviosa); so marca consecuencia (el hermano la animó, entonces caminó hacia el actor).

4 · Reading comprehension20 puntos

Read the text and answer the questions with complete sentences.

Last March, fourteen-year-old Tomás and his sister Mía were staying at their grandparents' house near a river in Entre Ríos. One night there was a terrible storm. It was raining very hard and the wind was blowing. At about midnight, while everybody was sleeping, Tomás heard a strange noise. He looked out of the window and saw that the water was coming into the street. He woke up his family and they all went upstairs. Then Tomás saw his neighbour, an old man, at the door of his house. The man couldn't walk fast, so Tomás and his grandfather used a small boat to help him. A few days later, a local newspaper wrote an article about Tomás. Today he usually helps at the fire station on Saturdays because he wants to be a firefighter.

  1. Where were Tomás and Mía staying?
  2. What was the weather like that night?
  3. What was everybody doing when Tomás heard the noise?
  4. What did Tomás see from the window?
  5. Why did they use a boat?
  6. Did a newspaper write about Tomás?
  7. What does Tomás usually do on Saturdays? Why?
  8. True or false? Correct the false sentence: «The storm started in the afternoon.»

Ver clave del reading
  1. They were staying at their grandparents' house near a river in Entre Ríos.
  2. There was a terrible storm. It was raining very hard and the wind was blowing.
  3. They were sleeping.
  4. He saw that the water was coming into the street.
  5. Because the old man couldn't walk fast and there was water in the street.
  6. Yes, it did. A local newspaper wrote an article about him.
  7. He usually helps at the fire station, because he wants to be a firefighter.
  8. False. It was at night: Tomás heard the noise at about midnight.
  9. Past continuous: were staying, was raining, was blowing, was sleeping, was coming. Irregulares: heard, saw, woke up, went, wrote.
  10. Consequence (consecuencia): como no podía caminar rápido, lo ayudaron con un bote.
